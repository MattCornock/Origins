### Cuppa Turca — 2026-09-25

- **Address:** 262 High St, Northcote VIC 3070
- **What it is:** Turkish dessert bar known for künefe and dondurma (stretchy Turkish ice cream, made fresh daily, vegan options available). Floor seating with cushions and mosaic tiles.
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 6pm–10pm · Fri 6pm–11pm · Sat–Sun 3pm–11pm
- **Links:** [cuppaturca.com](https://www.cuppaturca.com/locations-1) · [AGFG listing](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/cuppa-turca-67702)

**Rating:** 7/10, would go back
